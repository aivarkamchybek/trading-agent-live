Never tested — where the next experiment comes from

  • Momentum days with a news catalyst drift; days without it reverse.
    mechanism: Underreaction to news · proxy: an 8-K filed between the previous close and the signal close (edgartools)
  • A weak close (IBS < 0.25) improves the pullback entry.
    mechanism: Short-term overreaction · proxy: (close - low) / (high - low) < 0.25
  • Entries are worse when price is already extended above its 50-day.
    mechanism: Greed and FOMO · proxy: (close - sma50) / ATR(14) above a threshold
  • The assumed 0.1%/0.6% round-trip costs understate the real spread.
    proxy: journal fill price vs the scan price at that check, over 20+ trades
  • Scaling the stop and target by ATR reduces drawdown without cutting return.
    proxy: stop = entry - k*ATR(14), k in {1.5, 2, 2.5}

2026-10-23 · note

The dashboard now states the sample size a verdict needs

Plan 1.4: the minimum track record length (Bailey & Lopez de Prado 2012) replaces 'about 20 trades'. At +0.4% a trade with a 3.5% spread it is about 210 closed trades; paper had 2 when this went in.

2026-10-23 · note

The risk gate now checks the broker, not only the journal

Plan part 2, before the Robinhood connection places a real order. book.py blocks any order while account.json and the journal disagree (book.py reconcile); a buy on a scan over 20 minutes old, under the $1 minimum, without a recent equity baseline, or more than 1.5% (stocks) or 3% (crypto) from Robinhood's own quote; and a sell with neither an exit signal nor a news verdict. The trade-check skill carries the order lifecycle: no duplicate orders, review or preview first, no stock buy after 15:55 ET, poll then cancel, journal the actual fill.

state.json shows the end-of-day check missed on every weekday from 10-14 to 10-22 (7 of 9 days), although cron-job.org is meant to dispatch it at 15:40 and 15:50 ET and dispatched runs are not dropped. 2026-10-16 · finding

At Robinhood's real cost, crypto momentum has no measurable edge

Robinhood discloses about 0.95% per side on default routing, 1.9% a round trip; every backtest assumes 0.6% (paper.py now charges the disclosed 0.95% a side). At 1.9% crypto MOMENTUM averages -0.06% a trade over 5 years (n=1943, 95% CI -0.59% to +0.48%) and -0.08% over 3, and breaks even at about 1.84%. The live-rules table above still uses the assumed 0.6%, until realized costs are measured. See research/validation/audit_2026-10-16.md.

2026-10-16 · finding

Stock momentum's out-of-sample failure was measured on a different rule

The walk-forward's -0.21% scores the variant each fold picked, never the live rule. Scored directly on the same five test windows, the live rule makes +0.16% a trade (n=582, 95% CI -0.23% to +0.54%), and +0.21% over 5 years (CI -0.00% to +0.42%). Positive, but neither interval excludes zero: unproven rather than failed, and the owner kept the rule (CLAUDE.md). The table above still shows the walk-forward pick. See research/validation/audit_2026-10-16.md.

2026-10-14 · finding

The universe expansion appears to have invalidated part of the rejection table

Murphy's 'close above the 20-day high' was rejected on the old 50-stock/10-coin universe (crypto +0.75% vs +1.11%). On the current 200/100 universe it beats the live rule: +1.56% vs +1.21%, DSR 0.622 vs 0.193. The same pattern holds for 'beating SPY/BTC over 100 days' on stock PULLBACK (+0.50% vs +0.35%). Re-running the whole rejection table on the current universe is now the highest-value cheap experiment.
